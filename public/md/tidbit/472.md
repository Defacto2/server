TCS BBS was an early "elite" bulletin board and online platform for the PC from 1989 that was used in North America. And while a number of scene personalities of the era are linked to this software, it is hard to determine its origins or the legitimate authors. The source code is in Turbo Pascal 5.

Of the listed staff,

- Barimor, Renegade Bithead, Lord Blix, Lord Zombie, The Viper, all became early members of [The Humble Guys](/g/the-humble-guys).
- Barimore of [The Firm](/g/the-firm), also operated [The Final Frontier](https://demozoo.org/bbs/3453/) BBS.
- The Viper and Lord Blix both of The Firm operated [Tinseltown Rebellion](https://demozoo.org/bbs/4688/) BBS.
- Lord Blix also ran [The Mudd Club](https://demozoo.org/bbs/5286/) BBS.
- Lord Zombie was part of the [ESP Headquarters](https://demozoo.org/bbs/4729/) BBS.
- While there is a Mastermind who ran [Beta Cygnus](https://demozoo.org/bbs/8919/), a BBS affiliated with The Firm.
