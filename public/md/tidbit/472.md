TCS BBS was an early "elite" bulletin board and online platform for the PC from 1989 that was used in North America. And while a number of scene personalities of the era are linked to this software, it is had to determine its origins or the legitimate authors.

Only the source code survives today and it looks to be in Turbo Pascal 5.
