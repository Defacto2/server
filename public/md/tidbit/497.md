**Ritz Crackers** was likely named after the [snack food](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ritz_Crackers) of the same name. The one release we have is signed _Cracked August 1987 by Bilbo Baggins of Ritz Crackers_. [Biblo](/p/bilbo-baggins) was closely associated with the group Boys from Company C and Ritz Crackers may have been the prototype name for BCC. However, the release suggests this was Bilbo's first crack. It maybe BCC was the older brand originally used for sharing wares, and that only later became a cracking group.

Further evidence suggesting a link is the 1990 list of [HackroNyms](/f/b825909) written by Rad Man, later founder of [ACiD Productions](/g/acid-productions) writing:

```
BCC - 1. Boys from Company C
      2. Bilbo's Cracking Club
```
