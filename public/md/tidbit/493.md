The Cellar Elite were a mid-eighties group comprising of Apple II crackers who released on the IBM PC. We are unsure if there were any packaged releases of games, but they did release a number of cracking guides, game cheats, and even a cracking magazine titled KRACK that was first published in April 1986. In the text releases, two names are always used, Captain Quazmo and Reset Vector, but never together. So it maybe they are the same individual.
