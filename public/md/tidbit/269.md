**Console News** is a newsletter authored by [Caffeine](https://demozoo.org/sceners/91101/) who claims it was the Console Scene's first disk-mag and longest magazine.

Machines mentioned in the issues:

- 3DO
- Arcade
- CD-i
- Jaguar
- Konix multi-game
- Mega CD
- Genesis/Mega Drive
- NeoGeo
- Saturn
- Super NES and Famicom
- Project Reality
- PSX

Issue one is said to be released on the 8th of June 1993 is mostly summaries of industry press releases.

Issue 6 from February 1994 talks about editor Caffeine and his BBS being busted by Sega.

Issue 7 from April 1994 interviews the following:

- [Hawkeye](https://demozoo.org/sceners/59012/) of Crystal.
- [P0T-NOoDLE](https://demozoo.org/sceners/1432/) (pot-noodle) of Anthrox.
- J/\y (jay) of Humane and Vanity.
- Paranoid of Humane and Vanity.
- Bren of Alpha Flight.
- Numb.
- [The Great Muta](https://demozoo.org/sceners/124896/) of [New Japan Pro](https://demozoo.org/groups/126842/) (NJP) and NSC.
- [B¡TSTaR&ZeRo](https://demozoo.org/groups/2696/) (bitstar and zero).
