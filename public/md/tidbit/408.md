**Reaction** once misused the names **Forgive Us Crazy Kids** and F.U.C.K. Console.
They took the names knowingly without any permission of the original group.
And after being told off for using it, Reaction [agreed](/f/ad1ccb5) to rename themselves under the umbrella of **Blood, Guts an' Disorder** or BGD, and **Reaction** became the console division.

- Aug, 1993 - FUCK releases Ultimate PC-SNES Utility Pack
- Apr, 1994 - The Unknown Gamer writes, _incase you didn't know yet (Where the F.U.C.K. have YOU been?),
  Couriers of Corruption has changed its name to (the new) Forgive Us Crazy Kids! .. So, I decided to use the name of this extinct group. All previous members of FUCK are welcome in the new FUCK._
- Sep, 1994 - The Unknown Games writes, _The original FUCK had died LONG ago. I, being the unoriginal person I am (according to several people), stole the name when the group was alive._
