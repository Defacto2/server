K-Rad & Elite was a release chart magazine covering the Amiga microcomputer in 1995, arguably very late in the platform's life-cycle. Authored by [Raj](https://demozoo.org/sceners/20697/) of [Prestige](https://demozoo.org/groups/1434/), it was mostly a solo effort by a Scener who was in-between high school and University, whom presumably had a lot of free time. Raj! opening issue is a little obnoxious, _i look at these mags and see charts for demos, intros, muzack, grfx... all crap to me.. i am a pirate... so i make this new chart for every month based on the PIRATE scene_.

In mid-1995, Raj! moved onto the PC with a confusing issue #4, **PC edition** which scores games, trainers, cheats, fixes, and doc releases. The second PC issue for August split the magazine into two sections, games released from floppy disks and those ripped from CD. Even by this period, the number of floppy disk titles were shrinking drastically.

There's only two known issues covering the PC, but there were at least a couple of further releases following the Amiga Scene where Raj comments on its dire state, which in hindsight is more of a reflection of the platform. _our favorite reviewer, WiCKED!, decided to leave the scene for a while, seeing as how dead it is_; _Same story with Sizzler, our AGA reviewer, who has decided also to quite the scene._; _PSG is one of, if not THE best group out there right now, to see yet another one bite the dust, when we are so close to an amiga revival and therefore life again in the scene (years from now yes heh)._
