A number of people wrote to [bbsdocumentary.com](http://software.bbsdocumentary.com/IBM/DOS/FORUM/) to confirm **TCS** and **Emulex** are derivatives of the [Forum-PC](/g/forum_pc-bbs) source code from 1986 or 1987. They also state that TCS was heavily influenced by Emulex authored by [Sam Brown](/p/sam-brown) and [Shadow Lord](/p/shadow-lord). Given this, I'd speculate that the two guys behind Emulex went on to fork the TCS code by branding it as _TCS/2 v1.50_. However, once the TCS authors learned this, they reasserted their ownership by releasing _TCS v1.50 ½_. Leaving the Emulex pair to instead move on to create _Emulex/2_.
