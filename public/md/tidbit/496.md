The release of Jungle Hunt has a number of credits embed into it, but it seems the originator is Mr. Big Bopper and it was probably done in 1984 or 1985. It is also likely he came up with the name **Leechsoft** as a play on Atarisoft, the publisher of Jungle Hunt who were a major player in the early and mid-1980s.

Elsewhere in the program file are two, _Fuck all Copyrights All Rights Reserved_ that replaced "(C) 1983 Atari, Inc. All Rights Reserved". And the original "(c)1984 Atari All Rights Reserved" notice that is displayed on the game title screen has been swapped with _Cracked By Mr. Big Bopper!_

It seems in 1987, The PTL Club either repackaged, or re-cracked the earlier release by Mr. Big Bopper, injecting their own customized game loader and crack screen. The PTL loader was modified from code developed by [SPI](/g/software-pirates-inc) and advertises _This has been yet another quality game brought to you by the members of the PTL Club. Look for more PTL Club cracks! Call the Buccaneer BBs (312) 560-77XX_. And, _PTLLoad from SPI, decoded by [Sam Brown](/p/sam-brown) and modified by [Bad Brains](/p/bad-brains)_.
